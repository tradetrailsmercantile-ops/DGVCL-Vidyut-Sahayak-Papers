YouTube is being treated as a **recovery lead**, not as proof by itself that an original PDF is available.

## Important limitation

A solution video can be extremely useful for:
- locating the original paper PDF through the description
- identifying paper date/set
- comparing individual question wording
- recovering questions when the original PDF is no longer indexed

But a solution video alone is not treated as an original official paper. When a video reproduces every question clearly, it can support a **reconstructed/question-content copy** for research, but the repository will label that separately from an original PDF.

## Source rule

Never classify a video that merely mentions DGVCL in tags/description as the requested DGVCL Electrical Assistant paper. The actual title, exam date, post, paper content, or linked source must support the identification.
