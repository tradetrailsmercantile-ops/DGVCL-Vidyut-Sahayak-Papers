# Search Log

Research started: 2026-09-30

## Search principles

1. Search DGVCL-specific terms first.
2. Search spelling variants: Vidyut/Vidhyut, Electrical Assistant/Electrician Assistant, Ex-Apprentice.
3. Check contemporaneous Gujarat exam archives.
4. Separate DGVCL from PGVCL, MGVCL, UGVCL, GETCO and GSECL.
5. Separate Electrical Assistant from Junior Engineer (JE) and Junior Assistant (JA).
6. Treat a later allotment/result year as evidence of the underlying exam date, not automatically as a new paper.

## Confirmed first-pass exam events

### 2016
Found: DGVCL Vidhyut Sahayak (Electrical Assistant), 02-10-2016. The MaruGujarat archive explicitly lists a question paper with revised answer key.

### 2017
No DGVCL Electrical Assistant paper found in the first search pass. This is an open search item, not evidence that no exam occurred.

### 2018
Found: DGVCL Vidyut Sahayak (Electrical Assistant), 22-04-2018. RIJADEJA and MaruGujarat both identify the event and report question paper + official answer key.

### 2019
Found: DGVCL Ex-Apprentice to Vidyut Sahayak (Electrical Assistant), 11-08-2019.

### 2020
No distinct DGVCL Electrical Assistant written paper found in the first pass. A 2020 DGVCL allotment record exists, but it does not establish a new 2020 written test. PGVCL 2020 papers were deliberately excluded.

### 2021
Found: DGVCL Vidyut Sahayak (Electrical Assistant), exam 24-10-2021. Four paper types A/B/C/D and corresponding answer keys are reported. A contemporaneous page exposes DGVCL-hosted answer-key URLs.

### 2022
2022 DGVCL allotment records explicitly refer back to the written test held on 24-10-2021. Therefore 2022 is not counted as a separate written paper yet.

### 2023
Found an additional DGVCL Ex-Apprentice Electrical Assistant written test on 19-03-2023 from result/allotment records. The first-pass search did not locate its question paper. This is a high-priority missing-paper target.

### 2024
Search results surfaced DGVCL JE papers, not Electrical Assistant papers. JE is excluded from the primary archive.

### 2025
No verified DGVCL Electrical Assistant paper located in the first-pass search.
