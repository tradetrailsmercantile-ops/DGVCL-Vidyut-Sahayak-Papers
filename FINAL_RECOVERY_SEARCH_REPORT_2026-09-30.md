## Important distinction

- FOUND: question-paper source identified
- VIDEO_PAPER_LEAD / VIDEO_SOLUTION_FOUND: video lead, not an original PDF
- EVENT_CONFIRMED / PAPER_NOT_FOUND: exam documented but paper not recovered
