# Cross-DISCOM Electrical Assistant Paper Strategy

## Scope update

The research archive now treats DGVCL, PGVCL, UGVCL and MGVCL Vidyut Sahayak (Electrical Assistant) papers as a shared preparation/research corpus, while still keeping each paper labeled by its original company, exam date, recruitment route and paper/set.

This does not mean that every company used the exact same question paper in every year.

Evidence from 2021 shows distinct Electrical Assistant written-test events:
- MGVCL CBT: 27-06-2021
- DGVCL Ex-Apprentice EA CBT: 15-07-2021
- UGVCL written test: 25-07-2021
- DGVCL regular EA exam: 24-10-2021, Paper Types A-D

Therefore the correct model is:

Same/closely related Vidyut Sahayak (Electrical Assistant) cadre + related technical syllabus/pattern -> useful cross-DISCOM corpus

but

different company/date/recruitment event -> not automatically the same paper.

## How we will use other DISCOM papers

### Tier 1: Exact DGVCL EA papers
Highest-priority evidence for identifying DGVCL-specific question patterns.

### Tier 2: PGVCL / UGVCL / MGVCL EA papers
Use for:
- additional previous-year questions
- repeated technical concepts
- recurring question wording
- syllabus coverage
- likely question-bank overlap
- solution videos and paper reconstruction

### Tier 3: Related Gujarat technical exams
GSSSB Wireman and similar exams can be used as a secondary technical cross-reference, but they must never be presented as DGVCL/PGVCL/UGVCL/MGVCL Electrical Assistant papers.

## Verification labels

- ORIGINAL_PDF: actual paper bytes recovered and checked
- OFFICIAL_ANSWER_KEY: official/company-hosted answer key recovered
- SECONDARY_MIRROR: third-party copy/link
- VIDEO_RECONSTRUCTION: questions recovered from a video/solution when the original PDF is unavailable
- EVENT_ONLY: exam/recruitment event documented but paper not recovered
- EXCLUDED: wrong post, wrong company, or otherwise outside the relevant EA corpus

## Practical consequence

When searching for a missing DGVCL paper, we will no longer stop at DGVCL-only searches. We will also search PGVCL, UGVCL and MGVCL Electrical Assistant archives and YouTube solution videos for:
1. identical or near-identical questions,
2. the same recruitment cycle or common question bank,
3. mirrored PDF copies,
4. clues about paper codes, dates and answer-key filenames.

Every result will retain its original company identity so that cross-DISCOM evidence is not accidentally misreported as DGVCL evidence.
